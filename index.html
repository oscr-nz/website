What OSCR actually does

No bloat. Just the parts of the job that take up your time.

Jobs your way

Name your own workflow stages and add your own fields. Change a job's status in one click, and overdue work is highlighted so nothing slips.

Photo progress updates

Snap photos as the work goes and text the customer a private progress page. It shows your business name, not ours, and disappears after 30 days.

Texts on your terms

Quotes, final prices and "ready to collect" texts, in your own wording. Nothing goes to a customer until you press the button.

Scan it, find it

Scan a serial number or barcode with a phone or a USB scanner to fill it in, or to pull up every job that item has had.

Works away from the bench

Scan a job's QR label with your phone to update it or add photos. No signal? Photos wait on the phone and upload once you're back in range.

Parts & stock, only if you need them

Parts on order are tracked and marked arrived when they're delivered, with low-stock alerts for the shelf. Don't sell physical things? Switch it off.

Customers remembered

Every job links to a customer with their full history. Start typing a name and returning customers fill in automatically.

Bring your old records

Import customers, past jobs and stock from a spreadsheet or your current system's export, and undo it in one click if it's not right.

Xero invoicing

Mark a job paid and a draft invoice appears in Xero, ready for you to check and send.
