What OSCR actually does

No bloat. Just the parts of the day that actually take up your time.

In the workshop

Jobs your way

Name your own workflow stages and custom fields. Click a job's status to change it, and anything due today or overdue stands out on the dashboard.

One-tap job shortcuts

Set up your common jobs once, like a basic service. One tap adds the work, the labour at your rate and the parts, taken off the shelf.

Customers check themselves in

A QR code on the counter. Customers scan it, fill in their details and what they've brought in on their own phone, and it lands in OSCR ready for you to confirm.

Photo progress updates

Snap photos as the work goes, even away from the shop, and send the customer a private progress page by text. It shows your name, not ours, and disappears after 30 days.

Texts on your terms

Quotes, final prices and "ready for pickup" texts go out when you press the button, in your own wording. Nothing is sent without you choosing to.

Customers remembered

Every customer has their full history, what they own and how they ride. Start typing a name or number and it fills in.

Work coming in from your website

Online bookings

A booking page for your own website. Customers pick a day and a morning or afternoon slot, you approve each one, and they get an email in your shop's name. They pay when they collect.

Online quote requests

Customers pick a service, answer the questions you'd ask and add photos. You adjust the price and send a PDF quote in your own layout, and they accept it online.

Hire and rentals

Gear out by the half day, day or week. Condition photos out and back, who has what and when it's due, and servicing flagged by the days each item has been hired.

See everything OSCR does

At the counter

The till is part of the same system

Sell off the shelf, ring up a finished job, or both on one sale. Stock comes off as it sells, the customer's history is right there, and the day adds up at close.

OSCR records card payments taken on your existing EFTPOS terminal. Your terminal stays exactly as it is.

Sales, refunds and lay-bys

Scan a barcode or search stock. Split payments across cash, EFTPOS, bank transfer, gift card or account.

Receipts and end of day

GST receipts on a standard 80mm receipt printer, and a cash-up that shows what should be in the drawer.

Gift cards

Sell, top up and redeem, at the till or on a job. Printable cards with a barcode, and expiry set to at least 3 years, as NZ law requires.

Accounts and store credit

Let regulars put jobs on account with a limit you set, and give store credit that's spent like money.

Pay by bank transfer

Your account number and a job reference can go on the final price text and lay-by receipts, so customers pay from their banking app. You mark it paid when it lands.

Stock and suppliers

Supplier invoices, read for you

Drop in a supplier's PDF invoice and OSCR reads the codes, quantities and costs into stock. A new layout? Show it the columns once and it remembers.

Reordering without the guesswork

Low stock grouped by supplier, purchase orders in a click, and deliveries checked off against what you ordered.

Parts for a job, tracked

Order parts against a job and OSCR follows the parcel, so you know the moment they've been delivered.

Customers and behind the scenes

Events and group texts

Group customers, say everyone with an e-bike, and text them about a sale or an event. Only people who've agreed to texts, with a stop link in every message.

Xero invoicing

Each paid job and till sale becomes a draft invoice in Xero, parts and labour itemised, ready for you to check.

Backups and updates, handled

A full backup every night, copied to a USB drive or cloud folder if you like. New versions install with one click, and step back on their own if anything's wrong.

Find anything fast

Every search box suggests matches as you type, whether it's customers, stock, gift cards, past sales or settings.

One price, your whole team

No per-user fees. Everyone gets their own login, and owner-only settings stay locked behind the owner's password.

Runs on your own PC

Your data stays in the shop. Phones and other computers on the shop Wi-Fi open it in a browser.

Moving from another system?

Export a spreadsheet from what you use now and bring it straight in. OSCR matches the columns for you and shows what it found before anything is saved, and any import can be undone.
