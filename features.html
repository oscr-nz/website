Your kind of business

OSCR speaks your trade, not software

You pick a business type when you set up. OSCR changes the words on every screen, sets up sensible workflow stages, and switches on only the features that trade needs.

Workshop & repair

Bike shops, mechanics, phone and computer repair. Things come in, get fixed, go out. Jobs, parts on order, the till and tickets for the bench.

Field & outdoor

Landscaping, lawns, fencing, farm work, building, cleaning. Each client's sites on file, materials against the job, and photos from the phone on site.

Service & studio

Designers, media, photographers, consultants. Client projects with deadlines and standard packages, and no stock screens in the way.

Rental & hire

Bikes, e-bikes, tools, trailers, any gear. Out by the half day, day or week, with condition photos and servicing counted by days hired.

Stock & orders

Small retail and makers. The till, inventory, reordering and supplier deliveries up front, with customer orders tracked through.

Two businesses, one counter

A workshop and a hire company sharing a shop computer? Each gets its own OSCR, with its own books, staff and subscription, and a button to switch between them.

A workshop gets Repairs A landscaper gets Jobs, Sites and Materials A studio gets Projects and Clients A hire shop gets Hires and Hire items
Jobs

From booked in to paid and picked up

The dashboard opens on what needs you: overdue work, what's due today, and parts that have landed.

Your own workflow stages

Name the stages your work really goes through, and add custom fields for the details your trade needs. Click a job's status to move it along.

One-tap job shortcuts

Set up the jobs you do every week once, like a basic service. One tap adds the work, the labour at your rate and the parts, taken off the shelf.

Photos, notes and files

Everything about the job in one place: what was found, what was done, before and after photos, and any paperwork.

Quotes and final prices

A quote and a final price on every job, with Paid or Unpaid at a glance, and labour worked out from your hourly rate.

Customers check themselves in

A QR code on the counter. Customers scan it, fill in their details and what they've brought in on their own phone, and it lands in OSCR ready for you to confirm.

The archive

Finished work moves out of the way but stays searchable, with every past job against the customer when they come back.

Customers

Keeping people posted without the phone calls

Nothing is ever sent automatically. Every message goes when you press the button, in your own wording, under your business's name.

Texts on your terms

Quotes, final prices and "ready for pickup" in one tap. Texts are charged by the message, not bundled into the monthly price.

Photo progress updates

Snap photos as the work goes, even away from the shop, and send the customer a private progress page by text. It shows your name, not ours, and disappears after 30 days.

Customers remembered

Full history against every customer: what they own, what you've done for them, what they've bought. Start typing a name or number and it fills in.

Events and group texts

Group customers, say everyone with an e-bike, and text them about a sale or an event. Only people who've agreed to texts, with a stop link in every message.

Parcel tracking

Parts ordered for a job are followed to the door, so you know the moment they've been delivered rather than chasing a courier.

Find anything fast

Every search box suggests matches as you type, whether it's customers, stock, gift cards, past sales or a setting you can't remember the name of.

Online bookings

A booking page for your own website

Put a link or a button on your site and customers book themselves in. Nothing is confirmed until you say so, and no booking fees come out of it.

They pick a day and a slot

Your items and prices, morning or afternoon, your days off blocked out, and how far ahead people can book. It won't take a booking for something already out.

You approve every one

Requests land in OSCR. Accept or decline with a message, and the customer gets an email either way, from your business's name with your pickup details.

Pay when they collect

No card details and no deposits to handle. The booking turns into a hire or a job on the day with the details already filled in.

It says your name, not ours

Your business name and wording throughout, with a quiet "Powered by OSCR" and nothing else of ours on the page.

Online quotes

Quote requests that arrive already answered

Built for the trades where quoting eats the evenings: landscapers, contractors, studios. A "Get a quote" page for your website that asks what you'd ask.

You set the questions

Pick your services and the questions that decide the price: how many square metres, how many hours, yes or no extras. Each one carries a price you set.

Photos from the customer

They add photos of the lawn, the hedge, the wall. You see the job before you've driven anywhere.

An estimate on the spot, if you want

Show them a number straight away, or keep it to yourself and just say you'll be in touch. Your call, per shop.

You adjust, then send

The request arrives with their answers, photos and your estimate. Change the lines, add a note, and send the quote.

A PDF in your own design

Three layouts, your colour, your logo, your payment details and terms, and your own quote numbering. It attaches to the email and downloads from the quote page.

They accept online

One button on their phone. It shows as accepted in OSCR, and one more click turns it into a job with the customer, the price and the photos already in place.

At the counter

The till is part of the same system

Sell off the shelf, ring up a finished job, or both on one sale. Stock comes off as it sells, the customer's history is right there, and the day adds up at close.

Sales, refunds and lay-bys

Scan a barcode or search stock. Split payments across cash, EFTPOS, bank transfer, gift card or account.

Receipts and end of day

GST receipts on a standard 80mm receipt printer, and a cash-up that shows what should be in the drawer.

Gift cards

Sell, top up and redeem, at the till or on a job. Printable cards with a barcode, and expiry set to at least 3 years, as NZ law requires.

Accounts and store credit

Let regulars put jobs on account with a limit you set, and give store credit that's spent like money.

Pay by bank transfer

Your account number and a job reference go on the final price text and lay-by receipts, so customers pay from their banking app. You mark it paid when it lands.

Xero invoicing

Each paid job and till sale becomes a draft invoice in Xero, parts and labour itemised, ready for you to check.

Your EFTPOS terminal stays exactly as it is. OSCR records card payments taken on the terminal you already have. There's nothing to swap out and no new merchant agreement.
Stock and suppliers

Knowing what's on the shelf and what's coming

Supplier invoices, read for you

Drop in a supplier's PDF invoice and OSCR reads the codes, quantities and costs into stock. A new layout? Show it the columns once and it remembers.

Reordering without the guesswork

Low stock grouped by supplier, purchase orders in a click, and deliveries checked off against what you ordered.

Parts for a job, tracked

Order parts against a job, follow the parcel in, and confirm which shelf or bin they went on so nobody hunts for them.

Low stock on the dashboard

Set a reorder level per item and OSCR tells you before you run out, rather than when a customer asks for it.

Costs and margins kept straight

Cost and sell price on every item, updated as invoices come in, so the price on the shelf keeps up with what you paid.

Customer orders

Something ordered in specially for someone is tracked against them, so it's not sold to the next person through the door.

Hire

Gear out the door and back again

For anyone renting things out: bikes and e-bikes, tools, trailers, equipment. Half day, day or week, with your own prices for each.

Your fleet, by type

Set up hire types once with their usual prices, then add the items. Sizes and rider heights where it matters, so the right one goes out.

Condition photos out and back

A few photos as it leaves and as it returns, dated and kept against that hire. New damage is easy to see and hard to argue with.

Servicing by days hired

OSCR counts the days and hires on each item and flags it for a service at your interval, rather than you keeping it in your head.

Out now, at a glance

Who has what, when it's due back, and what's overdue. Check it in with a note and any damage in a couple of taps.

Payment on the till

Send a hire straight to the till to take payment, alongside helmets, locks and anything else they're buying.

A QR hire form

Customers fill in their own details and what they want on their phone, and OSCR suggests the item that fits them.

Labels and tickets

Everything in the shop has a code on it

Print a ticket or a label, stick it on, and scan it into any search box later to open the right job or item.

Job tickets, four ways

A wrap tag round a handlebar or tube, a flat ticket, a hang tag with a hole for a cable tie, or a big box label for a parts tub. On a standard 80mm receipt printer.

Labels on a label printer

DYMO, Brother or Zebra, with a dozen common label sizes built in and your own measurements if yours isn't one of them.

QR and barcode on every one

Scan the QR with a phone or the barcode with an ordinary USB scanner. Either opens the job, the part or the hire item.

Scan into the sale

Scan a job's ticket at the till and it joins the sale at its final price, so nothing gets rung up wrong.

Receipts in your branding

Your logo, address, phone and GST number on till receipts and tickets, with a footer in your own words.

Labels for parts and hire gear

The same code on a part waiting on a shelf or a bike in the hire fleet, so anyone can find what they're holding.

Running it

On your computer, looking after itself

Runs on your own PC

Your jobs, customers and photos live in the shop, not on someone else's server. Phones and other computers on the shop Wi-Fi open it in a browser.

Backups and updates, handled

A full backup every day, copied to a USB drive or cloud folder if you like. New versions install with one click, and step back on their own if anything's wrong.

One price, your whole team

No per-user fees. Everyone gets their own login, and owner-only settings stay locked behind the owner's password.

Tutorials built in

A "?" on every page with a short walkthrough that points at the real buttons. New staff pick it up without you standing over them.

The internet dropping doesn't stop you

OSCR keeps working for three days without a connection. Only texts, bookings and online quotes need the internet at all.

Nothing of yours leaves the shop

Texts and bookings pass through our server because they have to. Customer names, job details and notes never do, and nothing is sold or shared.

Moving from another system? Export a spreadsheet from what you use now and bring customers, job history, stock, gift card balances and store credit straight in. You check the columns before anything is saved, and any import can be undone.
